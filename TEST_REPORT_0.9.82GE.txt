IMPLEMENTED BEHAVIOR
--------------------
1. Auto Battle checks the selected target before using Servant Weapon - Phantom (5204)
   or Servant Weapon - Demolition (5205).
2. If the target lacks servant_sign, Auto Battle inserts Servant Weapon - Sign (5203).
3. A successful Sign does not advance the four-slot attack rotation. The next combat tick
   returns to the original configured Phantom/Demolition skill.
4. Target status is evaluated per monster. Changing targets requires a new Sign.
5. If Sign is unlearned, on cooldown, blocked by SP/resource requirements, or the combined
   resource is insufficient, the chain is skipped in favor of another skill or normal attack.
6. Auto Battle reserves at least one servant after Sign and skips zero-servant Phantom casts,
   so it does not waste SP on an attack with no useful hit.
7. Automatic missing-mark failures do not write repeated system messages. Manual use still
   displays the normal guidance message once.
8. Sign is allowed on Boss/MVP targets.
9. Resource preview for mode=up_to uses the configured minimum instead of demanding the
   maximum amount, allowing the remaining 1-4 servants to power the follow-up correctly.
