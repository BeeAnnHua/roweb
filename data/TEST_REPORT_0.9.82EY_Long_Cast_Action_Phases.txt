RO_WEB 0.9.82EY — Long Cast Action Phase Contract
===================================================

User-visible fix
----------------
- A cast bar no longer loops the complete six-frame Cast animation two or three times.
- A cast-time Attack no longer stretches the entire weapon Attack cycle across the whole bar.

Runtime contract
----------------
1. Cast begin: play only the preparation frame segment once.
2. After the preparation segment reaches its final frame, hold that ready pose until the cast bar completes.
3. Cast completion / effect settlement: play only the final release segment once.
4. Six-frame Cast assets use frames 0-3 for preparation and 4-5 for release.
5. Eight- or nine-frame Attack assets reserve the final three frames for release.
6. Instant skills keep the existing complete action-cycle behavior.
7. 0.9.82EX motion classification remains authoritative and unchanged.

Implementation
--------------
- player_atlas_runtime.js now supports prepare/release frame windows and hold-within-duration behavior.
- skill_engine.js sends castPhase=prepare at cast begin and castPhase=release at completion.
- The existing animation handoff still prevents paySkillCost() from replaying a duplicate full action.

Regression targets
------------------
- Magic cast-time damage: Attack prepare -> Attack release.
- Physical cast-time damage: Attack prepare -> Attack release.
- One-second-plus Buff: weaponless Cast prepare -> weaponless Cast release, exactly once each.
- Character atlas segmentation: 6-frame Cast = 4+2; 9-frame Attack = 6+3.
